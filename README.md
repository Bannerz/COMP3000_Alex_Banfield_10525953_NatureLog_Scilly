# NatureLog Scilly

## Design and Evaluation of a Citizen Science Application for Machine-Learning-Assisted Species Identification and Wildlife Recording in the Isles of Scilly

NatureLog Scilly is a mobile citizen science application for the residents and visitors of the Isles of Scilly who want to participate in wildlife recording. 

It is designed to address the problem of limited confidence in species identification and difficulty interpreting wildlife observation data.

The application helps users identify and record plants and animals though machine-larning-assisted identification, and explore sightings through accessible maps and visualisions to learn about their local environment.
